How TourChain keeps you safe

AI Safety Monitoring

Our AI engine continuously analyzes your location patterns. If you remain inactive in an unsafe area or deviate significantly from your plan, it triggers an AI Anomaly alert for the police.

Blockchain Immutable Logs

Every major event (Journey Start, Panic Button, Journey End) is recorded on the Polygon blockchain. This ensures a tamper-proof digital record for legal and safety verification.

Instant Panic Button

The floating red shield is your lifeline. One tap alerts the central local police dashboard immediately with your live GPS location and notifies your emergency contacts.

Family Safety Circle

Add family members to your profile. When you activate a journey, you can choose who to include. Authorities retrieve these contacts instantly during emergencies.

Itinerary Safety Score

When planning a trip, destinations are assigned a Safety Score based on police records and real-time risk data, helping you choose the safest stops for your vacation.
